MW4 Texture Streaming Toolkit - start here

1. Close the game.
2. Double-click "MW4 Texture Streaming.exe". The window offers a short guided setup: say yes.
3. Follow the checklist at the top. It finds your game folder (you confirm it), then you press Start session and start the game.
4. When you are done: Stop session.

Something wrong? Open the Doctor tab, or double-click "Restore hosts.bat" to undo the only system change this tool makes.
More: docs\GETTING-STARTED.md (plain words), docs\USAGE.md (everything).
